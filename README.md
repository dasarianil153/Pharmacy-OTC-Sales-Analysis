# Pharmacy-OTC-Sales-Analysis
A Power BI dashboard project analyzing over-the-counter (OTC) pharmacy sales across products, countries, and salespersons. This project demonstrates end-to-end data cleaning, KPI creation, and interactive dashboard design.
📊 Project Overview

- This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of OTC pharmacy sales performance using transactional data. It helps business users:

- Track total revenue and sales volume

- Identify top-performing products and salespersons

- Analyze sales trends over time

- Compare revenue across countries

- Understand product contribution to total revenue

🎯 Key KPIs

- Total Sales Revenue – Sum of Amount ($)

- Total Boxes Shipped – Sum of Boxes Shipped

- Average Revenue per Box – Total Revenue / Total Boxes

- Number of Transactions – Count of sales records

🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop

- Microsoft Excel (for data inspection)
  
- GitHub (version control & portfolio hosting)

🔍 Insights Generated

- Digestive Enzyme and Antiseptic Cream are the top revenue-generating products.

- USA and UK contribute the highest share of total revenue.

- Sales peak around May and July.

- A small group of salespersons drives a major share of total revenue.
